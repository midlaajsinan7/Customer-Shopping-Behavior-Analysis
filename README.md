# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer shopping behavior using transactional data from 3,900 purchases across multiple product categories. The goal is to uncover insights into customer spending patterns, product preferences, discount usage, customer segmentation, and subscription behavior to support data-driven business decisions.

The analysis combines **Python, Pandas, MySQL, and Power BI** to prepare customer data, perform business analysis, and present actionable insights through an interactive dashboard.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze revenue contribution across customer demographics.
- Identify high-rated products and popular items within each category.
- Examine discount usage and identify high-spending discount users.
- Compare average spending and revenue between subscribers and non-subscribers.
- Analyze customer purchasing behavior across shipping types.
- Segment customers based on previous purchase history.
- Investigate repeat buyers and their subscription status.
- Analyze revenue contribution across different age groups.
- Present key findings through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Python** – Data cleaning, transformation, and exploratory data analysis
- **Pandas** – Data manipulation and missing-value handling
- **MySQL** – Database integration, querying, and business analysis
- **Jupyter Notebook** – Python analysis and documentation

## 📊 Key Analyses

### 1. Customer Revenue Analysis

Compared revenue generated by male and female customers to understand their contribution to total revenue.

**Key Findings:**

- Male customer revenue: **$157,890**
- Female customer revenue: **$75,191**
- Total revenue: **$233,081**

Male customers contributed approximately **67.7% of total revenue** in the dataset.

### 2. High-Spending Discount Users

Identified customers who used discounts and still spent above the overall average purchase amount.

The analysis identified **839 purchase records** meeting the query criteria, helping examine spending behavior among discount users.

### 3. Product Rating Analysis

Ranked products based on their average review ratings to identify highly rated items.

| Product | Average Rating |
|---|---:|
| Gloves | 3.86 |

| Boots | 3.82 |
| Hat | 3.80 |

The overall average review rating was approximately **3.75 out of 5**.

### 4. Shipping Type Comparison

Compared average purchase amounts between Standard and Express shipping.

| Shipping Type | Average Purchase Amount |
|---|---:|
| Standard | $58.46 |
| Express | $60.48 |

Express shipping had an average purchase amount approximately **$2.02 higher** than Standard shipping. This is an observed association and does not establish that shipping type causes higher spending.

### 5. Subscriber vs. Non-Subscriber Analysis

Compared customer counts, average purchase amounts, and total revenue across subscription status.

| Metric | Subscribers | Non-Subscribers |
|---|---:|---:|
| Purchase Records | 1,053 | 2,847 |
| Average Purchase Amount | $59.49 | $59.87 |
| Total Revenue | $62,645 | $170,436 |

Subscribers represented approximately **27% of purchase records**. However, their average purchase amount was slightly lower than that of non-subscribers, suggesting an opportunity to investigate subscription engagement and retention.

- **Targeted Marketing:** Use age-group and customer-segment insights to guide marketing analysis.
- **Shipping Strategy:** Investigate the relationship between shipping preferences and purchase amounts before making pricing or service decisions.
